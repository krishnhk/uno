        📖 How to play UNO

        🎯 Goal

        Be the first to get rid of all your cards. Everyone starts with 7.

        🕹️ On your turn

        1. Play a card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol, or play a Wild.
        2. Can't play? A card is drawn for you. If it fits, choose Play it or Keep it; either way the turn moves on.
        3. Down to one card? Press UNO! before the next player moves. Miss it and it's too late: on your turn you can't play your last card and must draw instead.
        4. Every move has 10 seconds. If time runs out, a card that fits is played for you; if none fits, a card is drawn for you and the turn passes.

        📣 Say UNO!

        When you're down to one card, press UNO! before the next player moves. Forget, and another player can press Catch! first: you draw 2 cards. If nobody catches you, you still can't go out: once the next player has moved, you must draw a card on your turn.

        🏁 Game modes

        Play till the end: players finish one by one; the last one holding cards loses.

        First out wins: the game ends when one player empties their hand; the rest are ranked by points left.

        The host picks the mode in the room before starting. Stacking (+2 on +2, +4 on +4) is an optional house rule.

        🏆 Scoring

        When you go out, you score the cards still in other players' hands.

        Numbers: face valueAction: 20Wild: 50
